Privacy Policy

Windowfit collects no personal information.

What the app reads

  • The position and size of the front window, only when moving it

When the app goes online

  • Checking for updates — once a day it reads the list of versions on GitHub (github.com). Nothing that identifies you is sent. You can turn it off in Settings.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings such as shortcuts, in macOS preferences. They never leave your Mac.
  • Your license key and trial start date (or, on a Mac that has used Windowfit since it was free, a mark saying so), in the macOS keychain. The license is checked inside the app, without going online.

When you buy

Payment is processed by a reseller. They handle your card details; we never receive them, and their privacy policy applies. We learn only what is needed to issue your license.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visits (such as IP addresses). We use no analytics, ads or cookies.
