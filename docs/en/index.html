What it does

Halves ⌃⌥← → ↑ ↓

Left, right, top, bottom. Press again to cycle the width: 1/2 → 2/3 → 1/3.

Quarters and thirds

⌃⌥U I J K for the corners, ⌃⌥D F G for thirds, ⌃⌥E T for two thirds.

Maximize, center, restore

⌃⌥↩ to maximize, ⌃⌥C to center, ⌃⌥⌫ to put it back.

Across displays

⌃⌥⌘← → moves the window to the next display, resized to fit.

From the panel

Click a picture in the menu bar panel; no shortcuts to remember.

Gaps if you like

0–30 pt around windows. Every shortcut can be changed.

With other tsubomi apps

With other tsubomi apps such as Profilink and TouchRotator running, the menu bar icons can be combined into a single 🌸.
